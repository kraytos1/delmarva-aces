🎬 Broadcast overlay
Applies to the live scorebug within about three seconds — no need to touch the YoloBox.
Sponsor logo beside the bug
Rotates through your active sponsors during play.
Sponsor card between innings
Plays three sponsors on every half-inning change.
Logo rotation speed
How long each sponsor holds beside the bug.
40s
Base diamond
The runners-on graphic in the scorebug.
Screen corner
Where the bug and sponsor plate sit in frame.
A URL switch on the scorebug (like ?sponsors=off) is a hard override and still beats these.
⚾ This game no live game
Saved on the game itself, so they only apply while one is being scored.
Pitching line
Pitcher name, pitch count and last velocity.
At-bat line
Current batter and their line for the day.
📋 Scorer prompts
All four are optional already — ignoring one costs nothing. Turn a prompt off to stop it appearing at all. Takes effect on the next game you start.
Base check
Confirms where runners ended up after a ball in play. Fixing it corrects the player's RBI, not just the scoreboard.
Fielder tag
Credit the defence on an out — this is what creates highlight clips for glove-first kids.
Spray capture
Tap where the ball landed. Builds spray charts on player pages.
Dropped third strike
Offers to convert a strikeout when the catcher didn't hold it.
